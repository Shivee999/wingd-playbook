Play it safe — the paperwork stack

Every licence, registration, contract and renewal a Mumbai restaurant is normally expected to hold. Tick them off as you go; progress saves on this device.

Read this before acting This is an operational checklist, not legal or tax advice. Licence names, thresholds, fees and applicability differ by ward, premises type and headcount, and Indian food and labour rules change frequently — FSSAI slabs and validity rules were revised in 2026. Confirm every item with a licensed CA and a local liaison consultant, and verify FSSAI details directly on the FoSCoS portal before filing.
